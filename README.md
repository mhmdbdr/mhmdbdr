### 🧑‍💻 About Me

Self-taught frontend developer from **Egypt 🇪🇬**, passionate about building fast, beautiful, and accessible web experiences.

I started my journey with **Vue.js & Nuxt.js** and have since evolved into a **React & Next.js** developer with a strong foundation in **TypeScript**. I care deeply about clean code, great UI, and smooth user experiences.

When it comes to animations — I'm obsessed. Whether it's a subtle micro-interaction or a full **GSAP** timeline, I believe motion is what separates good UIs from *unforgettable* ones. **Framer Motion** & **GSAP** are my playgrounds.
